About The Real Times

We are Zambia’s independent digital newsroom — delivering fact‑based journalism, stories that matter, and a voice for the people. Born in Lusaka, with a global perspective.

Truth, integrity, and the Zambian spirit.

Our story & mission since 2020

Founded in Zambia

The Real Times started in Lusaka with a small team of passionate journalists. We wanted to create a digital newspaper that reflects the reality of Zambia and the African continent.

Our mission

To provide accurate, balanced, and independent news that empowers citizens, holds power accountable, and celebrates the rich diversity of Zambia.

Our vision

To become the most trusted digital news source in Africa — built on integrity, innovation, and a deep connection to our communities.

What we stand for
  • Independence
  • Truth & accuracy
  • Zambian voice
  • Diversity & inclusion
  • Innovation
  • Community engagement
